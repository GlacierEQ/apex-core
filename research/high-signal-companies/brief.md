# Research Brief: High-Signal Company Architecture

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Question:** Select 6-8 high-signal companies (aerospace, defense, AI/chips, space) and architect full-cycle APEX solutions for their specific engineering bottlenecks.

**Scope:**
- Companies: NASA, Lockheed Martin, Boeing, NVIDIA, SpaceX, Northrop Grumman, Raytheon, BAE Systems
- Focus: Real engineering bottlenecks these companies face (not marketing)
- Output: Architecture proposals using APEX patterns (IPC mesh, epistemic gates, ML memory, metal engine, scrubber, gateway)

**Assumptions:**
- These companies have massive data processing, simulation, security, and orchestration needs
- APEX patterns (lock-free IPC, epistemic verification, Hebbian memory, SIMD compute) are directly applicable
- Full cycle = research bottleneck → architect solution → code skeleton → verification gate

**Depth:** Deep (5-8 sub-agents, 25+ sources)

## Angles

1. **Space & Launch** — NASA/SpaceX: telemetry processing, mission-critical verification, real-time simulation
2. **Defense Aerospace** — Lockheed/Boeing/Northrop: sensor fusion, classified data handling, supply chain
3. **AI Chips** — NVIDIA: GPU orchestration, model training pipelines, CUDA optimization
4. **Defense Electronics** — Raytheon/BAE: radar signal processing, EW systems, C4ISR
5. **Security & Compliance** — All: ITAR/EAR compliance, classified network isolation, audit trails
6. **Digital Engineering** — All: digital twin, simulation, MBSE (Model-Based Systems Engineering)
7. **Swarm Intelligence** — Defense/Space: drone swarms, satellite constellations, autonomous systems
8. **Cross-Cutting** — Shared bottlenecks: data loss prevention, real-time IPC, verified execution
